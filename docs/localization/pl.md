Modifier keys keep English names: ⌘ Command, ⌃ Control, ⌥ Option, ⇧ Shift.

## Form of address and style
- Second person singular (ty), as in macOS Polish: "kopiujesz", "Czy chcesz…" avoided in favor of infinitive questions ("Usunąć tablicę…?").
- Menu items and buttons: imperative (Usuń, Otwórz, Wklej). Toggles: imperfective imperative ("Pokazuj…", "Ignoruj…", "Otwieraj…").
- Clipsy as subject: avoid gendered past forms ("przeniosła"); use impersonal "-no/-to" forms.
- Sentence case everywhere; no English Title Case.

## Punctuation and plurals
- Quotes: „…” (U+201E / U+201D). Ellipsis "…" kept. Path separator " › " kept.
- CLDR plurals: one (1), few (2–4, 22–24…), many (0, 5–21, 25…), other (fractions).
