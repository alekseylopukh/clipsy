## Form of address
- "vous", imperative in vous form ("Copiez", "Autorisez"), as macOS does.
- Titles, menus and buttons in sentence case (French Apple style).

## Punctuation
- Quotes « » with U+202F narrow no-break space inside.
- U+202F before ? ! ; and U+00A0 before :.
- Typographic apostrophe ’, ellipsis …, path separator › as in English.
- Plurals: CLDR one/other; "one" covers 0 and 1.
